This Privacy Policy explains how Monetayz ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use our mobile application. Monetayz is a personal finance app that helps you track income, expenses, budgets, and recurring transactions.

We built Monetayz to keep your financial data private and under your control. This policy is written to be read in full — not skimmed — because it governs data about your money.

§ 01

Data we collect

1.1 Account information

When you create an account, our authentication provider (Clerk) collects your email address, an encrypted password (or your social sign-in identifier, if you use Apple/Google sign-in), and optionally your name and profile picture. We do not store your password ourselves — Clerk handles this on our behalf.

1.2 Financial data you enter

To provide the App's core features, we store the data you enter directly:

  • Transactions: amount, type (income/expense), category, date, and any note you add.
  • Categories: custom categories you create (name, icon, color).
  • Budgets: monthly spending limits you set per category.
  • Recurring transactions: templates for rent, subscriptions, salary, etc. (amount, category, frequency, start date).
We do not collect your bank account, card numbers, or any banking credentials. Monetayz is a manual expense tracker — it does not connect to your bank and never has access to real transactions beyond what you type in yourself.

1.3 App preferences

Theme (light/dark), currency, notification settings, and onboarding status, so your preferences follow you across devices.

1.4 Subscription status

If you purchase a Pro subscription, our payment processor (RevenueCat, via the Apple App Store or Google Play) tells us whether your subscription is active and which product you purchased. We never see or store your payment card details — those are handled entirely by Apple or Google.

1.5 Information we do not collect

  • We do not use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.
  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or other apps on your device.
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever.
§ 02

How we use your data

  • To provide the core functionality of the App (recording and displaying your transactions, budgets, and recurring items).
  • To sync your preferences and data across your devices when you're signed in.
  • To send you local notifications you've opted into (see Section 9).
  • To verify and manage your Pro subscription.
  • To respond to support requests you send us.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the service (e.g., rate-limiting abusive requests).

We do not use your financial data to train machine learning models, and we do not use it for advertising purposes.

§ 03

Legal basis for processing

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), our legal basis for processing your data under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is:

  • Performance of a contract — processing is necessary to provide the App's features to you.
  • Consent — for optional features like notifications, which you explicitly enable.
  • Legitimate interest — for basic security and abuse prevention.

If you are located in Türkiye, we process your data in accordance with the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK). Your rights under KVKK are listed in Section 6.

§ 04

Third-party services we use

We rely on a small number of trusted service providers (sub-processors) to operate the App. We do not sell or rent your data to any of them, or to anyone else.

Provider Purpose What they see
Clerk Authentication (sign-in/sign-up) Email, name, profile picture, authentication tokens
Neon Database hosting (PostgreSQL) Your transactions, categories, budgets, recurring rules, and preferences
RevenueCat Subscription management Subscription/purchase status linked to your account ID
Apple App Store / Google Play Payment processing Your payment details (we never see these)

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data on our behalf, and we have agreements in place requiring them to protect your data and use it only to provide services to us.

§ 05

Data storage & retention

Your data is stored in our database (hosted by Neon) for as long as your account remains active. You can delete individual transactions, categories, budgets, or recurring rules at any time within the App.

If you delete your account, all of your data — transactions, categories, budgets, recurring rules, and preferences — is permanently and immediately deleted from our database. Your authentication account (managed by Clerk) is deleted at the same time.

Before deleting your account, you can export a complete copy of your data as a JSON file from Settings → Export My Data.

§ 06

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Export your data in a portable, machine-readable format (available directly in the App: Settings → Export My Data).
  • Correct inaccurate data (you can edit most data directly in the App).
  • Delete your data and account (available directly in the App: Settings → Delete Account).
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent at any time for optional features like notifications.

Most of these rights are self-service within the App. For anything else, contact us using the details in Section 11 and we will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law (generally 30 days under GDPR, 30 days under KVKK).

§ 07

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) between the App and our servers, authentication tokens that expire and are verified on every request, and access controls that ensure your data is only ever returned to your own account.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we work to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

§ 08

Children's privacy

Monetayz is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA, where applicable), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

§ 09

Notifications

Monetayz can send you local, on-device notifications (a daily reminder and a large-expense alert) that you opt into in Settings. These are scheduled directly on your device using your device's operating system — they are not push notifications, we do not use a push notification server, and we do not store a device push token.

§ 10

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the App. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
